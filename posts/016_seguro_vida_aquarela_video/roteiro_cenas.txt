IMPORTANTE SOBRE DIREITOS AUTORAIS
-----------------------------------
A letra da música "Aquarela" (Toquinho/Vinícius de Moraes) NÃO entra nos
prompts. A música tem direitos autorais, e o Kling geraria personagens
cantando/falando a letra dentro do vídeo — isso pode derrubar o post e
não é algo reproduzível aqui. Em vez disso, este roteiro usa falas
ORIGINAIS, com as mesmas imagens/metáforas da música (nave, mar, tempo,
chuva e arco-íris, estrada, passarela colorida), ligadas ao seguro de
vida. A música original pode ser adicionada depois, pela biblioteca de
áudio do próprio Instagram, na hora de postar.

Formato: 6 cenas de 10s cada (~1 minuto no total)
Config. para todas as cenas: 9:16 · 10s · Áudio nativo ligado · 1080p

-----------------------------------------------------------------
CENA 1 — A nave sobre o mar
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Photorealistic, vertical. A sleek silver spaceship glides slowly over a
real ocean at dawn, slightly wobbling as if hard to steer. Waves roll
below, golden light on the water. Camera tilts up from the waves to the
ship. A calm, warm male narrator says in Brazilian Portuguese: "Ninguém
recebe o manual do futuro. A gente aprende a pilotar no caminho." Sound
of waves and a soft hum of the ship. Cinematic, 35mm, light haze.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
CENA 2 — O tempo não espera
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Photorealistic, vertical. Macro close-up of an antique brass pocket
watch on wet sand, its hands spinning fast. A wave covers it with foam
and pulls back. The same calm male narrator says in Brazilian
Portuguese: "O tempo não pede licença. E não espera a gente ficar
pronto." Sound of ticking mixed with crashing waves. Golden hour, slow
motion droplets.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
CENA 3 — Rir ou chorar
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Photorealistic, vertical. A woman in her 40s stands at the edge of the
sea. Half the sky is light rain, half is sunny, a rainbow appears over
the ocean. Her eyes are wet, then she smiles. She says softly in
Brazilian Portuguese: "Tem dia que a vida faz a gente rir. Tem dia que
ela pede coragem." Sound of light rain and waves. Slow push-in,
emotional, cinematic.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
CENA 4 — A estrada na névoa
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Photorealistic, vertical. A narrow sandy road along the coastline
disappears into thick sea fog, waves crashing beside it. Low drone shot
moving forward along the road. The calm male narrator says in
Brazilian Portuguese: "Ninguém sabe onde essa estrada vai dar." Sound of
wind and distant waves. Blue-gray tones, mysterious.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
CENA 5 — A passarela colorida
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Photorealistic with painterly color. A family walks hand in hand along
a long runway made of vivid watercolor paint stretching across the
beach toward the sea: a father, a mother and a young child. The father
looks at the child and says in Brazilian Portuguese, warm and smiling:
"Mas eu sei com quem eu quero caminhar." The child laughs. Sound of
waves and laughter. Tracking shot from behind, golden light.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
CENA 6 — As cores que se apagam (fechamento)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Photorealistic, vertical. Aerial shot slowly pulling back from the
family on the watercolor runway. The colors gently fade and wash into
the ocean foam, but the family keeps walking together, holding hands.
A silver spaceship passes far in the sky. The calm male narrator says
in Brazilian Portuguese: "O futuro a gente não controla. Mas quem a
gente ama, a gente protege." Soft waves, peaceful. Poetic, cinematic.
